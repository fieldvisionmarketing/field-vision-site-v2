  - question: "How do I launch a content app?"
    answer: "Launch a content app in three moves. First, pick one beachhead audience and give them a reason to show up on a specific date. Second, seed the content and creators before launch so the first users find something worth staying for. Third, design the first week: identify the two or three behaviors that predict a user will return, and build onboarding and messaging that drives them. At Pandora, two first-week behaviors predicted long-term retention, and building around them lifted week-two retention 21%. Paid acquisition comes after you know who stays."
  - question: "How do I get my first 10,000 users for a content app?"
    answer: "Your first 10,000 users usually come from concentrated communities. Recruit the creators, artists or personalities your audience already follows and give them a reason to bring fans. Show up where that audience gathers: Discord servers, subreddits, fan accounts and live events. Build sharing into the product so every new user becomes a distribution channel. Save paid acquisition until your day-7 retention proves the users you buy will stay."

A fractional CMO typically embeds 2 to 3 days per week until the team can run the system on its own."
