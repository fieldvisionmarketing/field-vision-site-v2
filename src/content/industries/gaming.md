faqs:
  - question: "What does a fractional CMO for a gaming startup do?"
    answer: "A fractional CMO for a gaming startup is a senior marketing executive who embeds with your team part-time, typically 2 to 3 days per week, or for a defined launch project. For a sportsbook, prediction market, or game studio that means launch strategy for each new market or title, player acquisition tuned to retained value, activation and retention systems, brand positioning against larger incumbents, and the team and vendors to run it. Field Vision builds these systems and transfers them to your team."
  - question: "How do you market a sportsbook launch in a new state?"
    answer: "Treat each state as its own launch window. Build awareness before go-live, concentrate media, partners, and cultural moments into the first weeks, and localize messaging for the market. Then shift fast to activation: the behaviors that predict long-term value, such as a second bet within 48 hours, multi-sport betting, and live in-play wagering. At Hard Rock Bet this approach supported six state launches and beat acquisition forecasts by 232%."
  - question: "How is marketing a prediction market different from a sportsbook?"
    answer: "Prediction markets reach a broader audience than sports bettors, since users trade on news, politics, culture, and the economy as well as sports. That widens the content and creator opportunity, and it raises the stakes on trust, clear explanations of how markets work, and marketing that holds up as regulation evolves. The launch and retention fundamentals are shared with sportsbooks: own the window, then build habit after the first trade."
  - question: "Do you work with mobile and video game companies too?"
    answer: "Yes. Game launches share the same shape as sportsbook launches: a compressed window where attention and acquisition costs peak, then early sessions that decide retention. Field Vision works on launch strategy, positioning, creator and community programs, and the lifecycle systems that keep players coming back, drawing on Twitch's live gaming platform experience."
  - question: "Do you only work with funded gaming companies?"
    answer: "Field Vision works with gaming companies from seed through Series D. Earlier-stage teams usually need positioning, a focused launch plan, and their first retention loops; later-stage teams need a repeatable multi-market playbook, a stronger team, and tighter unit economics. Engagements can be a fractional CMO retainer or a defined go-to-market project around a launch."
