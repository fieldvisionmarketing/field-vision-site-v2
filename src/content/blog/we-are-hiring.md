## Summary

Field Vision is building a roster of senior, project-based integrated marketers who can operate as well-rounded GTM athletes. This role is for people who are comfortable flexing across marketing disciplines — from brand, to content, to growth — depending on what the client needs most. You will step into high-stakes client situations and deliver marketing plans that drive growth. Judgment, structure, and communication matter more than channel-level depth in any one area, as you will move fluidly between strategic and tactical matters. If you're interested in what drives our culture and how we think about building teams, [learn more about our approach](/about/).

## What You Will Do

- Operate as a senior, fractional/project-based marketing lead for top consumer tech clients
- Turn vague client challenges into clear marketing solutions
- Design integrated marketing plans that span across phases, channels, and tactics
- Author end-to-end GTM documents, creative and content briefs, and campaign plans
- Design growth experiments, hypotheses, and measurement approaches to validate direction
- Present work directly to clients and handle pushback live
- Own workstreams independently, flexing across disciplines as needed
- Partner tightly with internal client marketing teams
- Identify where lifecycle, onboarding, or retention mechanics can or should support GTM efforts

## What We Look For

- 5+ years experience marketing at consumer tech companies, start-ups, or agencies
- Demonstrated range across multiple marketing disciplines
- Strong strategic judgment under ambiguity
- Ability to move fast, respond rapidly, and not lose the plot
- Structured thinking and organization
- Seasoned communicators; strong ability to present and influence
- Comfort fielding questions from senior stakeholders and executives
- Ability to work independently, seamlessly partner with client teams, and elevate the Field Vision brand

## Bonus Points If

- You have worked in entertainment (sports, music)
- You have worked in streaming (video, audio)
- You have held both brand and growth roles in your career
- You can write copy and have a strong creative eye
- You are fluent in building AI workflows, tools, and agents

## About Us

Field Vision is a modern integrated marketing consultancy that partners with consumer-tech and entertainment brands during key inflection points. Our work spans [go-to-market strategy](/services/go-to-market/), integrated marketing, growth marketing, content strategy, and marketing operations.

The goal is always the same: help clients solve their marketing challenges and unlock business growth. Whether it's helping a brand launch like [Twitch did in the esports and music space](/case-studies/twitch/), or building the right [fractional CMO](/services/fractional-cmo/) relationship to guide their growth, we're looking for marketers who thrive in high-complexity, high-impact situations.

Field Vision is not a traditional agency — we do not operate as an AOR with bloated teams and a penchant for retainers. We work in focused, high-velocity engagements where we help teams define the right play, build the right systems, and then hand them the playbook so they can run it themselves.
