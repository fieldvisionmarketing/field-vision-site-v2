Most people daydream about starting their own agency. Shachar Aylon actually did it.

In this episode of Run The Play, Field Vision sits down with Shachar Aylon to break down how he walked away from big tech to build a creative agency from scratch — the risks, the reality, and the playbook behind making the leap. This conversation explores the [fractional CMO](/services/fractional-cmo/) model and how independent operators are reshaping how brands think about creative leadership.

## The journey from corporate to independent

Shachar's transition mirrors trends we're seeing across the industry. The [compound growth system](/guides/compound-growth-system/) that builds sustainable agencies relies on understanding both the creative and business sides. Shachar shares the hard lessons from the [Maestro case study](/case-studies/maestro/) and how that experience shaped his approach to building his own shop.
