faqs:
  - question: "What is growth marketing?"
    answer: "Growth marketing is a data-driven, full-funnel approach to customer acquisition, activation, retention, and expansion. Unlike traditional marketing that focuses on top-of-funnel awareness, growth marketing treats the entire customer journey as a connected system — from first touch through long-term retention and lifetime value optimization. It combines paid acquisition, lifecycle marketing, CRM automation, experimentation, and analytics into a unified engine. Growth marketing is especially critical for startups because it connects marketing spend directly to revenue outcomes and unit economics."
  - question: "How is growth marketing different from performance marketing?"
    answer: "Performance marketing optimizes individual channels for short-term conversion — typically focused on paid media metrics like CPA and ROAS. Growth marketing is a broader system that connects acquisition to retention, lifetime value, and expansion. It includes performance marketing as one component but also encompasses lifecycle marketing, CRM, product-led growth, experimentation, and unit economics modeling. The difference is scope: performance marketing wins short-term campaigns, while growth marketing builds a machine that compounds over time."
  - question: "What is lifecycle marketing?"
    answer: "Lifecycle marketing is the practice of delivering the right message to the right customer at the right stage of their journey — from onboarding through retention and win-back. It uses segmentation, behavioral triggers, and automated journeys to move customers through the funnel without manual intervention. Common lifecycle stages include activation (getting new users to first value), engagement (deepening usage), retention (preventing churn), and expansion (increasing lifetime value). Effective lifecycle marketing can reduce churn by 20 to 40 percent and significantly improve customer lifetime value."
  - question: "How quickly will we see results from growth marketing?"
    answer: "Early wins typically appear within 4 to 8 weeks — things like funnel optimization, CRM setup, and quick experimentation wins. Meaningful growth acceleration usually takes 3 to 6 months as testing compounds and retention improvements start showing up in cohort data. The timeline depends on your baseline: companies with existing data and infrastructure move faster than those building from scratch. We set clear milestones at each phase so you can track progress against expectations."
  - question: "How do you reduce customer acquisition cost while improving retention?"
    answer: "Reducing CAC while improving retention requires working both sides of the equation simultaneously. On the acquisition side, we optimize channel mix, improve conversion rates through experimentation, and build organic and referral channels that reduce dependence on paid media. On the retention side, we build lifecycle journeys, improve onboarding, and use cohort analysis to identify and fix the specific moments where customers drop off. When acquisition and retention are optimized together as a system, unit economics improve dramatically — which is why we take a full-funnel approach rather than treating them as separate problems."
