faqs:
  - question: "What is content marketing strategy?"
    answer: "Content marketing strategy is the planning framework that defines what content you create, who it is for, where it gets distributed, and how it connects to business outcomes. It includes audience research, content pillars, editorial calendars, format selection, distribution plans, and measurement frameworks. A strong content marketing strategy ensures every piece of content serves a purpose, whether that is driving search visibility, building audience trust, generating leads, or supporting customer retention. For startups in digital media and entertainment, content strategy is especially critical because your audience expects quality and authenticity."
  - question: "How long does it take for content marketing to show results?"
    answer: "Content marketing is a compounding investment, not a quick fix. Early indicators appear within 4 to 8 weeks, things like search indexing, initial traffic patterns, and audience engagement signals. Meaningful business impact typically takes 3 to 6 months as content builds search authority, audience trust, and pipeline influence. The timeline depends on your starting point: companies with existing content and audience see faster results than those building from scratch. We set clear milestones at each phase so you can track progress against expectations and see the momentum building."
  - question: "What is the difference between content marketing and creator marketing?"
    answer: "Content marketing is what your brand creates and owns, blog posts, videos, podcasts, newsletters, and social content. Creator marketing is partnerships with external creators whose audiences align with yours. They are converging rapidly in the digital media and creator economy space. A strong strategy uses both: your owned content builds trust and ranks in search and AI engines, while creator partnerships expand reach, add credibility, and tap into established communities. Most startups underperform on both because they treat them as separate functions instead of a connected system."
  - question: "How do you measure content marketing ROI?"
    answer: "Content marketing ROI is measured across multiple dimensions: search traffic and keyword rankings, engagement metrics like shares, comments, and saves, pipeline influence showing how many leads cite content in their journey, revenue attribution tracking customers who engaged with content before converting, and audience growth rates. We build a measurement framework that tracks all of these dimensions and ties them directly to business outcomes. Vanity metrics like page views and impressions do not matter, what matters is whether content is driving awareness, trust, and revenue."
