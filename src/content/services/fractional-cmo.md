faqs:
  - question: "What is a fractional CMO?"
    answer: "A fractional CMO is a senior marketing executive who works with your company on a part-time or contract basis, typically 2 to 3 days per week, providing the same strategic leadership as a full-time Chief Marketing Officer without the $500K+ salary, equity, and benefits package. Unlike consultants who advise or agencies who execute campaigns, a fractional CMO embeds with your team as an operating leader: owning strategy, building systems, managing vendors and team members, and reporting directly to the CEO and board. Fractional CMOs are especially common in the startup ecosystem, where companies between seed and Series D need experienced marketing leadership but are not ready for a full-time C-suite hire."
  - question: "How much does a fractional CMO cost?"
    answer: "Fractional CMO costs typically range from $5,000 to $15,000 per month depending on scope, time commitment, and the seniority of the executive. At Field Vision, engagements are structured as monthly retainers based on a 2 to 3 day per week commitment. This is significantly less than a full-time CMO, which runs $500K+ in total compensation when you factor in salary, equity, benefits, and recruiting costs. The exact investment depends on your specific needs, team size, and growth stage, which we discuss during the discovery call."
  - question: "How is a fractional CMO different from a marketing agency?"
    answer: "A marketing agency executes campaigns on your behalf. A fractional CMO leads your marketing function from the inside. The key difference is accountability and scope. An agency delivers assets and reports. A fractional CMO owns the strategy, builds the systems, hires the team, manages the vendors (including agencies), and is accountable for business outcomes, not just deliverables. A fractional CMO also builds internal capability so your team can eventually run independently, while agencies typically create ongoing dependency."
  - question: "How long does a fractional CMO engagement last?"
    answer: "Most fractional CMO engagements at Field Vision run 3 to 6 months, though some extend to 12 months or longer depending on complexity. Every engagement follows the ABCT® model, Audit, Blueprint, Construct, Transfer, which means there is a clear structure, defined milestones, and a planned exit point. The goal is always to build systems your team can own and operate independently. We are built to be fired, that is the measure of success."
  - question: "What types of startups benefit most from a fractional CMO?"
    answer: "Startups between seed and Series D see the strongest results from fractional CMO leadership, particularly those in digital media, streaming, entertainment, and the creator economy. Common triggers include preparing for a product launch, entering a new market, hitting a growth plateau, or scaling a marketing team without a senior leader. If you have a product with traction but marketing that feels tactical and disconnected from business outcomes, a fractional CMO provides the strategic layer you are missing."
