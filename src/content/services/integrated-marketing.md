faqs:
  - question: "What is integrated marketing?"
    answer: "Integrated marketing is the practice of aligning all marketing functions, brand, growth, content, product marketing, and customer experience, around a unified strategy and customer narrative. Instead of each function optimizing independently, they operate from a shared plan and shared goals. The result is a consistent customer experience across every touchpoint, more efficient spend, and compounding impact over time. For startups, integrated marketing means the difference between a collection of disconnected tactics and a coordinated system that drives growth."
  - question: "Why does integrated marketing matter for startups?"
    answer: "Startups have limited resources, which means every marketing dollar needs to work harder. When brand, growth, and content teams operate in silos, you get duplicated effort, inconsistent messaging, and wasted spend. Integrated marketing eliminates those inefficiencies by connecting every function around a single customer journey. At the Series A to B stage, this is especially critical, it is the inflection point where ad hoc marketing breaks down and intentional systems become the difference between scaling and stalling."
  - question: "How do you measure integrated marketing success?"
    answer: "Success metrics depend on where you are as a company. For early-stage startups, we measure time-to-campaign-launch, message consistency across channels, and team coordination efficiency. For scaling companies, we tie metrics directly to business outcomes: customer acquisition cost, lifetime value, retention rates, and revenue influence. We build a unified measurement framework during the Blueprint phase that connects brand, growth, and content metrics in a single dashboard, so you can see how each function contributes to the whole."
  - question: "How is integrated marketing different from hiring an agency?"
    answer: "Agencies execute campaigns on your behalf. We design the marketing system that tells agencies (and your internal team) what to execute and why. We embed with your team, build internal capability, and hand off everything we create. You are not dependent on us, you own the playbook and can evolve it as you scale. In fact, our work often makes agency relationships more productive by giving them a clearer brief and better measurement framework."
  - question: "Can integrated marketing work with a small team?"
    answer: "Integrated marketing works even better with smaller teams. The smaller your team, the more siloing kills efficiency, because you simply cannot afford to have three people working on disconnected initiatives. We compress the design phase for early-stage companies and focus on simple, scalable systems that a small team can operate with limited resources. The frameworks scale up as you grow, so you are building on a solid foundation from day one."
